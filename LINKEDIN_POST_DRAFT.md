Can a video game launch affect the resale price of a console? 🎮📈

I explored historical second-hand Xbox Series S (512 GB) pricing in Germany ahead of GTA VI's scheduled November 2026 launch, using Python, two marketplace datasets and time-series visualizations.

One finding: the median reported eBay sold price in my filtered sample rose from **$230.54 in August** to **$330.81 in November 1–18**—an increase of **43.5%**. A separate Kleinanzeigen dataset also showed higher asking prices, though the samples and currencies are not directly comparable.

The interesting part wasn't just plotting a rising line. It was handling missing historical data, detecting questionable product classifications, and asking whether Microsoft's August Xbox retail-price increase could also explain the pattern.

This is an **exploratory, observational analysis**, not proof that GTA VI caused the price increase. I've documented the assumptions, data-quality caveats, reproducible aggregate charts and next steps on GitHub.
